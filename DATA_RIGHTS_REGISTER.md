# PAMANA data rights register

| Source area | App use | Status | Required action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Travel Oriental Mindoro public place names | Curated Lakbay place cards | Source checked | Keep attribution, source URL, and verification date. |
| Travel Oriental Mindoro festival calendar | Offline festival learning cards and quests | Source checked / dynamic | Recheck dates and programs before each content release. |
| Travel Oriental Mindoro user reviews, photos, check-ins, and accounts | Not imported | Excluded | Do not copy or process without a separate rights and privacy agreement. |
| Travel Oriental Mindoro travel schedules and advisories | Not bundled as authoritative data | Dynamic | Add only through a maintained official feed or refresh workflow. |
| Mangyan Heritage Center institutional context | Source-linked review notes | Review pending | Request permission for any expanded reproduction or partnership content. |
| Mangyan group profiles and community geography | Review queue only | Community review pending | Obtain community review and FPIC before public educational use. |
| Ambahan text, translations, manuscripts, and recordings | Review queue only | Permission required | Obtain rights, community consent, creator attribution, and benefit-sharing terms. |
| Mangyan craft images and living designs | Review queue only | Permission required | Obtain image/design rights and community approval. |

## Review states

- `source_checked`: public factual information is linked to its source, but dynamic information still needs rechecking.
- `community_review_pending`: the record may describe living communities or cultural identity and must not be presented as final authority.
- `permission_required`: do not publish the material until written rights and consent are recorded.
- `do_not_publish`: restricted, personal, sensitive, or otherwise unsuitable for the public app.
